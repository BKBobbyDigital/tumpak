5700 S Lake Shore Dr, Hyde Park
One of the best science museums in the world — U-505 submarine, Coal Mine, Space Center, weather experiments. Felix will want to stay all day. Budget 2 hours and be disciplined about it.

5700 S Lake Shore Dr, Hyde Park
Steps from MSI, right on Lake Michigan. Felix can run it off while you decompress — then grab lunch nearby in Hyde Park before heading north for the party.
